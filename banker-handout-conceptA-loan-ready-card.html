The Loan-Ready Borrower Checklist

Lenders lend against confidence in the numbers. Here's what turns a borrower's financials into a fast yes.

1. The document set

What underwriting expects on the desk.

  • Three years of business tax returns
  • Three years of year-end financial statements
  • A current interim statement (most recent month)
  • A personal financial statement for each guarantor
  • A current debt schedule

2. The numbers behind them

What makes those documents credible.

  • Bank, credit-card & balance-sheet accounts reconciled to date
  • Books that tie out to the tax returns and bank records
  • A clean, consistent chart of accounts
  • Cash flow that's clear — not just revenue
  • No stale balances or unexplained entries

Books that don't check these boxes yet? That's exactly what we fix.
