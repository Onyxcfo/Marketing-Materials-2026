Bankable, or Not?

Five things that stall a loan — and what good looks like.

The red flagWhat good looks like →
1

Accounts that don't reconcile

→ Bank, credit-card & balance-sheet accounts reconciled right up to date.

2

Books that don't match the tax return

→ Financials that tie out cleanly to the returns and the bank records.

3

Revenue, but no clear cash flow

→ Cash flow you can see and project — lenders lend against it, not revenue.

4

Personal & business funds commingled

→ Clean separation, so the business's real numbers stand on their own.

5

Stale or unexplained entries

→ Current books with every balance supported and explainable.
