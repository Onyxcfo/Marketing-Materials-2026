## 1. Competitive read — how peers position, and where Onyx wins

I looked at comparable firms in two buckets:

**Local AZ peers** — Whyte CPA, Foster Financial CPA, Master Accounting & Tax, Beach Fleischman SOAR.
**National outsourced/fractional players** — NOW CFO, indinero, Hiline, Dark Horse, Pilot/Zeni-style platforms.

**What they consistently lead with:**
- A simple **flat monthly fee** promise ("one predictable monthly price").
- A **"focus on running your business, we'll handle the books"** benefit headline.
- Positioning the firm as a **strategic partner**, not just a bookkeeper ("we turn numbers into decisions").
- The **bookkeeper → controller → CFO "ROI ladder"** as a way to show you scale with the client.
- Named people and credentials (the better local firms), or polished brand + dashboards (the national platforms).

**Where Onyx can win — lean into these, because they're true and differentiated:**
1. **A team, not a solo bookkeeper or an anonymous app.** You have a Principal, a CFO, a Controller, and staff accountants. That means real review layers and segregation of duties — a genuine quality-and-trust advantage. (This is the professional, grown-up version of your current "don't be stuck with 1 accountant" idea.)
2. **Bookkeeping *and* CFO strategy under one roof** — clients don't outgrow you; they move up the ladder with the same team.
3. **Specific, named deliverables** (your service pages already prove this — most local competitors stay vague).
4. **Niche depth:** real-estate holding companies and multi-entity structures — say it plainly; it's a magnet for the right clients.
5. **Responsive, human service:** one-business-day response, available after hours — concrete and reassuring.
6. **Local + nationwide:** a real Scottsdale office *and* clients across the country.

**Net positioning line (the idea every page should ladder up to):**
*"A full accounting team — bookkeeping through CFO-level strategy — that grows with your business, for less than the cost of one in-house hire."*

---

## 2. Brand-voice guardrails (apply to all copy)

- **Keep:** warm, plain-spoken, confident, organized. Short sentences. Specific over clever.
- **Drop:** stacked analogies (pilot/cocktails, Wild West, rainstorm), fear-bait ("how you get audited"), and pop-culture asides (SNL/Chris Farley).
- **One light human touch is fine** ("sleep better at night" is good — it's reassuring, not jokey). Just don't stack four of them.
- **Lead with the client, not the firm.** "You get…" beats "Onyx provides…"
