## The strategic read (why this matters for the design)

The audience is **~5–6 commercial bankers** (Steven's host Tom plus his team), in a recurring 15–20 minute "invite a partner to pitch" slot. That changes the goal:

- These bankers are **not prospective clients — they're referral partners.** The win is becoming the firm they send their business clients to.
- A banker's job is getting good loans **through underwriting, fast.** What slows them down is borrowers with messy, un-credible financials.
- So the most "useful, not throwaway" handout is one that **helps the banker do their own job** — and makes Onyx the obvious fix when a borrower's books aren't ready.

What bankers actually need from a financial partner (per current lending guidance): reconciled books, statements that **tie out** to tax returns and bank records, **cash-flow** clarity (lenders lend against cash flow, not revenue), and the standard document set — 3 yrs tax returns, 3 yrs year-end statements, a current interim statement, and a personal financial statement per guarantor.

**Design language (2026):** calm and clean — strong typography, generous white space, one clear takeaway, one action. We're using an **"onyx & brass"** identity: near-black, warm paper, a restrained antique-brass accent. Premium, precise, and on-brand with the firm name.

## Concept A — "The Loan-Ready Borrower Checklist" ⭐ RECOMMENDED

**What it is:** A double-sided premium desk card. **Front** = a checklist of exactly what makes a borrower's financials loan-ready (the document set + what makes the numbers credible). **Back** = a short Onyx referral pitch and contact.

**Why bankers keep it:** It's a tool they'll actually use with every borrower — pin it to the wall or keep it in a deal folder. Every time they reach for it, they see Onyx.

**Why it wins for Onyx:** It positions Onyx as *the firm that delivers loan-ready borrowers* — the single most valuable thing you can be to a commercial banker. It's generous (helps them first), not salesy.

**Format:** 5×7 heavy stock (uncoated/matte, ~100 lb cover). A mockup of this concept is built — see the Artifact link in the chat.

---

## Concept B — "Bankable or Not? 5 red flags that stall a loan"

**What it is:** A one-pager (or same card format) listing the financial red flags that slow or sink a credit request — unreconciled accounts, books that don't tie to the tax return, revenue without cash flow, commingled personal/business funds, stale or unexplained balances — each with a one-line "what good looks like."

**Why bankers keep it:** It's a fast diagnostic they can mentally run against any borrower. Slightly punchier and more "teachable" than the checklist.

**Trade-off:** Frames problems rather than the positive document set. Strong as a *companion* to Concept A or as the PowerPoint backbone.

---

## Concept C — "Bookkeeper → Controller → CFO: what your client needs, and when"

**What it is:** A clean visual of the financial-maturity ladder — what each role does, and the signs a business has outgrown the previous stage (and is ready to borrow, scale, or raise).

**Why bankers keep it:** Helps them gauge a client's financial maturity and know *when* to refer. Also doubles as a great client-education piece.

**Trade-off:** More about Onyx's service tiers than the banker's immediate job — excellent, but a notch less "useful at the desk" than A. Pairs naturally with A on the back panel.

---

## Concept D — "2026 Arizona Financial Calendar" desk card

**What it is:** A reference card of Arizona TPT due dates plus key federal/state filing deadlines for the year, Onyx-branded.

**Why bankers keep it:** Deadline references have a very high keep-rate (they live on a monitor edge). Useful to the banker *and* their clients.

**Trade-off:** Highest keep-rate, but the weakest at positioning Onyx specifically as a *lending* partner. Best as a **leave-behind bonus** alongside A — not the lead piece.

---

## Recommendation

**Lead with Concept A** (Loan-Ready Checklist) as the primary handout — it's the cleanest fit for "useful, not throwaway" *and* it positions Onyx exactly right for this room.

If you want a two-piece set that feels generous without being wasteful:
- **Primary:** Concept A card.
- **Optional bonus:** Concept D (AZ calendar) as a small extra they'll keep year-round.
- **PowerPoint:** built around Concept B's red-flags (problem) → Concept A's checklist (solution) → how Onyx helps your pipeline.

## 6. Optional PowerPoint outline (15–20 min, ~7 slides)

1. **Title** — Onyx Accounting Group · "Delivering loan-ready borrowers." Steven Nikolov, Founder, Principal & CFO.
2. **Who we are** — a full accounting team (bookkeeping → controller → CFO), Scottsdale-based, serving clients nationwide. The team, briefly.
3. **The problem you see every day** — deals slowed by messy, un-credible borrower financials (the red flags).
4. **What loan-ready actually looks like** — the checklist (the document set + credible numbers that tie out).
5. **How we get a borrower there** — Cleanup → ongoing bookkeeping/controller → CFO-level forecasting for the credit request.
6. **Why refer us** — clean books, faster underwriting, a borrower who shows up prepared; we work alongside you and the client's CPA.
7. **Let's work together** — contact, the handout, and an offer to be a resource for any deal in their pipeline.

*Keep it conversational — bankers respond to "I make your deals easier," not a feature list.*
