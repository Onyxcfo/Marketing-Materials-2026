Bookkeeper → Controller → CFO

The financial-maturity ladder — what each role does, and the signs a client has outgrown the one below. It helps a banker gauge a client's stage and know when to refer, and it shows Onyx covers all three with one team.

A desk reference from Onyx Accounting Group

What does your client actually need?

Three stages of financial support — and when a business moves up.

Stage 1Bookkeeper

Keeps the books current — daily transactions, reconciliations, AP/AR, payroll support.

Outgrown when numbers are always late, or you can't trust them enough to decide.

↑ ready for a tighter, more reliable close

Stage 2Controller

A faster, cleaner month-end close, real internal controls, and monthly financials you can rely on.

Outgrown when you're borrowing, raising capital, or need forecasting and strategy.

↑ ready to turn numbers into strategy

Stage 3Fractional CFO

Forecasting, cash-flow planning, and the financing & investor strategy behind a credit request or a raise.

This is the stage where deals get done — and where a prepared borrower stands out.

One team, all three stages — so your client never has to start over.
